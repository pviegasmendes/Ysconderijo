## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS, no build step, same conventions as the sibling project `barberia-diogo`: `public/` is what Netlify publishes, fonts are self-hosted (no Google Fonts), photos ship as WebP with JPG fallback. Three language versions: Portuguese (Portugal) at `/` (main), English at `/en/`, Spanish at `/es/`. Confirmed by Pablo (Digital Montra), October 2026.

## Users

People deciding where to have dinner in or near São Brás de Alportel (Algarve, Portugal), usually on a phone, often the same day or a few days ahead. Three real groups, matching the three language versions:

- Portuguese locals and people from nearby Algarve towns (Faro, Loulé, Tavira).
- Foreign residents of the Algarve and holiday visitors, many British, often sent by a villa owner or a friend. They read English.
- Spanish visitors: a large share of the Google reviews are written in Spanish. They read Spanish.

Their job: understand what kind of cooking this is, check that it is open tonight (weekday evenings only), see roughly what it costs, learn that it is cash only, and book a table by phone.

## Product Purpose

The website of Restaurante Ysconderijo, a dinner-only fusion restaurant in the centre of São Brás de Alportel. The restaurant has no website today: its Google Maps listing links to its Tripadvisor page.

This build is an unsolicited proposal: a free draft made by Digital Montra (digitalmontra.pt) to show the owner before they decide. Success for the restaurant: a visitor calls and books a table. Success for the draft: the owner recognises their own restaurant in it and wants it.

## Positioning

The name is the truth of the place: an *esconderijo*, a hideout. A plain front in a back street near the Câmara Municipal opens into a dining room of rough stone arches, white walls and warm light, where carefully plated fusion cooking is served: a Portuguese and Mediterranean base with Asian accents (tuna tataki with sushi rice, pad thai, lamb chops with blueberry sauce). Reviewers repeat the same surprise: from outside you would never guess what is inside. Ranked no. 2 of 56 restaurants in São Brás on Tripadvisor; 4.7 on Google. Open only for dinner, Monday to Friday.

## Operating Context

- **Bookings:** by phone only; there is no online booking system. Reviewers advise booking ahead because it is often full. Walk-ins are sometimes seated if there is a free table.
- **Address:** Rua Gago Coutinho 45, 8150-151 São Brás de Alportel, near the Câmara Municipal (Town Hall). Tripadvisor lists the front as nos. 45/47. Plus code 5426+JJ. Coordinates 37.1515346, -7.8884257.
- **Phones:** mobile 967 139 381 (the number the restaurant itself publishes on Facebook and Tripadvisor) is the booking number on the site; landline 289 849 520 (Google) is also shown. Which one is primary is to be confirmed with the owner.
- **Email:** restauranteysconderijo@hotmail.com (from the restaurant's Facebook page).
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday 19:00–23:30; closed Saturday and Sunday (Google and Tripadvisor agree; Facebook staff ads say "folga sábado e domingo"). Always Lisbon time. An older directory listing (Tuesday to Saturday, 19:00–22:00) is outdated.
- **Payment:** cash only. The owner's own words on Tripadvisor: "Caros clientes, abrimos só para jantares. Não aceitamos pagamento com cartão." Many reviews mention it; the site must say it before people arrive.
- **Holidays:** the restaurant closes for a summer break announced on Facebook (in 2026: 29/06 to 06/07). Dates change every year.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Ysconderijo, spelled with a Y. "Restaurante Ysconderijo" in full.
- **Logo (as seen on Facebook):** a burgundy disc with a gold calligraphic script wordmark "Ysconderijo", two small stylised flowers, and bands of fine parallel gold lines. No vector original has been supplied; any version on the site is a redraw to be replaced by the original.
- **The room:** rough stone arches, white walls, white high-backed chairs, light wood tables, warm low light, a large painting of a tree against an orange sky. Gentle jazz in the background. Staff change the cutlery wearing white gloves.
- **Voice in Portuguese:** warm and courteous European Portuguese, polite form ("a sua mesa"), never Brazilian. The owner writes plainly ("abrimos só para jantares").
- **Voice in English and Spanish:** plain and warm, written for visitors and residents rather than translated word for word.
- **Credit:** the footer credits "Site criado por Digital Montra" (EN "Website by Digital Montra", ES "Web creada por Digital Montra"), linking to https://digitalmontra.pt/.

## Product Principles

1. **One tap from a table.** Every screen leaves the visitor one step from calling to book.
2. **Practical truth up front.** Dinner only, Monday to Friday, cash only, book ahead: say it before anyone arrives disappointed.
3. **Real over generic.** The restaurant's own room, dishes, words and guests; never stock imagery or invented claims.
4. **Three tables, one room.** Portuguese, English and Spanish carry the same facts with the same care.
5. **Easy to keep by hand.** Digital Montra maintains the site by hand, so menu, hours and prices stay simple to find and edit in all three files.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, touch targets of at least 44 px, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, no infinite animation. The restaurant is wheelchair accessible (entrance, seating and toilets); the site says so.
