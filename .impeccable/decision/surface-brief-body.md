# Surface brief: home page (PT `/`, EN `/en/`, ES `/es/`)

- **Scope:** the one-page website of Restaurante Ysconderijo, in three language files sharing one stylesheet and one script.
- **Visitor mode:** Persuade.
- **Audience and job:** diners choosing tonight's or this week's dinner, mostly on a phone; they need the kind of cooking, whether it is open tonight, rough prices, cash only, and a phone number.
- **Action:** call to book a table (tel: link). Secondary: directions in Google Maps.
- **Proof on hand:** Google 4.7 (423), Tripadvisor 4.6 (618) and no. 2 of 56 in São Brás, real review excerpts in each language, the printed menu (main courses and prices), three photos (room, prawn dish, tuna tataki).
- **Constraints:** no invented claims, no third-party embeds (no cookie banner needed), self-hosted fonts, WCAG AA, everything editable by hand in three files.
- **Memorable moment:** the plate is served: the place setting rises into view and its porcelain well opens into the menu.
- **Unresolved:** prices of the duck and chicken dishes (bracket placeholders), primary phone, owner's photos, domain.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is plated the way the Ysconderijo kitchen plates: one composition on a central axis, a wide dark rim of table around it, saffron sauce dots as leaders and a single balsamic swoosh, so the restaurant's most praised trait, its presentation, becomes the design system. It refuses the category default: full-bleed dark food photo under a centred serif title, two-column menu with dotted leaders, testimonial carousel, embedded map.

OWN-WORLD: Night-table ground #1A1617; porcelain #F6F4F1 as the reading surfaces; the logo's oxblood #5A2527 as the charger plate and the closing booking field; copper-tan #C99580 bands of three hairlines, taken from the logo, as the structure between courses; saffron #E2A93B dots graded large to small as leaders and live status; one balsamic swoosh on the page. Alex Brush wordmark (logo redraw), Bodoni Moda display and prices in tabular figures, Ysabeau Office text. Circles are plates; photos sit in round wells or tall rounded frames. No cards, no decorative shadows: the only shadow is a plate resting on the table.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor learns: a hidden, dinner-only fusion restaurant in São Brás, whether it is open tonight, and how to book by phone. Scrolling, they are served the menu as courses (Carne, Peixe, Vegan) with real prices, then the room (stone arches; from outside you would never guess), then guests' words and ratings, then the bill: hours with today lit, cash only, three steps to a table, address and phones. They call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 1440×900: an 80px bar with the wordmark capsule left, section links and PT EN ES right, phone pill. On the central axis: a two-line Bodoni H1 near 84px in porcelain; a 20px Ysabeau subline; the CTA row, four graded saffron dots leading into the porcelain pill "Reservar mesa · 967 139 381", with the live status line under it. Below, from about y 560, the place setting seen from above, oxblood charger with copper three-line bands, porcelain rim, prawn dish photo in the well, cut by the bottom edge. On a 390px phone the same axis holds: H1, status, pill, then the plate.

FORM: Empratamento, position 7 of 7 on my resonance list, assigned by the roll and chosen by Pablo. seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: on scroll the plate is served, rising and settling into place while its well opens with a circular clip-path into the porcelain menu section; with reduced motion it stands still.
